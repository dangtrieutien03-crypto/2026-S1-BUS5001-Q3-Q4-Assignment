## Observations

NotebookLM successfully generated summaries, risk analyses, briefing documents, visual artefacts, and audio explanations based on the uploaded sustainability report.

The summary and briefing functions were useful for reducing large volumes of information into concise academic outputs. The risk analysis capability assisted with identifying ESG-related themes and organisational risks from the source document.

The infographic and mind map features provided alternative visual representations of the report content, making key concepts easier to understand and communicate. The audio explanation feature supported content review through listening, which may improve accessibility and learning flexibility for students and researchers.

While the outputs were generally relevant to the uploaded document, users should still verify important findings against the original source material, as AI-generated content may occasionally omit details or oversimplify complex information.
